About This Project

This Indian Sign Language Translator is designed to help bridge the communication gap between the Deaf community and the hearing world.

Our vision is to create inclusive, accessible technology that empowers people, encourages learning, and promotes equal access to communication.

Built by students. Inspired by impact. Powered by compassion + technology.
